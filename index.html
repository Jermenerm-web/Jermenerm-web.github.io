Welcome to Pixie Stick’s!

Pixie Stick’s is a brand-new bakery made for people who believe dessert should be fun. Every week our bakers dream up fresh, colorful treats, so there is always something new to try. Whether you are stopping in for a quick snack, picking out something for the kids, or planning a birthday or celebration, you can find everything you need right here: the weekly creations, the full menu, and upcoming bakery events.
